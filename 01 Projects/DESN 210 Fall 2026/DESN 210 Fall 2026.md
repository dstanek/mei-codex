# DESN 210 Fall 2026

Adjunct teaching at CWRU — *Introduction to Programming for Business
Applications*. Python and algorithmic thinking for students in mostly
non-technical majors.

**One semester, one delivery, a definite end.** That completion condition is what
makes this a project rather than an Area — it started life in `02 Areas/` and was
moved on 2026-08-18 when the term began. Teaching *in general* is still ongoing
and has no note; each term gets its own project.

in Drive stays term-neutral because it's evergreen; this note is dated because it

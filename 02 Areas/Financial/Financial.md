# Financial

Ongoing responsibility, not a project — household finances have no completion
condition, which is why this lives in `02 Areas/` and carries no `todoist:` URL.
See *Areas* in [[99 Meta/Conventions]].

Discrete pushes with an end state — a refinance, a rollover, closing a dormant
account — become real Todoist projects. Everything else is upkeep and belongs in
`Personal › One-Off`.

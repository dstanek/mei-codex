# Goal Setting

The ongoing practice of setting and revisiting goals. No completion condition,
so it's an Area rather than a project and carries no `todoist:` URL. See *Areas*
in [[99 Meta/Conventions]].

Individual goals that need real work become their own Todoist projects with a
completion condition. The planning tasks that used to sit here — health and
financial goals — moved to `Personal › One-Off`.

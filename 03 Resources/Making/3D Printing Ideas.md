## Graduating an idea

A one-off print is not a project — just print it. Graduate only when it's a multi-step build:

1. **Todoist** — create the project under the `Personal` parent, then add a due-dated next action.
2. **Obsidian** *(optional)* — `01 Projects/{Short Name}/{Short Name}.md` from the `Project.md` template, with the Todoist URL in `todoist:`.
3. Replace the row here with a link to the project.

Everything smaller than that goes to `Personal` → `One-Off` with a due date, or nowhere at all.
